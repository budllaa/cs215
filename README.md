## Final Project Ideas

For my final project, I am working with my partner, Aerith.

We want to compare our personal Spotify listening histories and explore how similar or different our listening habits are.

Some questions we are interested in:

- How similar are the artists and genres we listen to?
- Do we listen to music at similar times of the day?
- How have our music tastes changed over time?
- Are there periods where our listening habits become more similar or more different?
- Do we replay songs and artists in similar ways?

We plan to use our Spotify listening history as our main dataset and may combine it with additional Spotify or music metadata.
